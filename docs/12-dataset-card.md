# 12. Dataset card and labelling guideline

Task: SAQ-20. State of `saqbol-ml/data/` as of 2026-10-22 (branch of PR #14, SAQ-17).

This document says where every row of the training data comes from, under which terms we may use
it, what the labels mean, and how new messages are labelled. It also states a limitation that
affects the numbers in [§9.3](09-verification-plan.md#93-current-baseline): we do not yet have
SMS messages that real people actually received.

## 12.4 What "real" means in the scripts

`train_baseline.py` and `evaluate.py` treat every row whose `source` does not start with
`synthetic` as a real message. That gives 228 rows: 184 from Spam-kazakh and 44 seed messages.

**None of these 228 is an SMS that a real person is known to have received.** The Spam-kazakh rows
read like examples written for the course project ("Бүгін ауа райы өте керемет.", "Сәлем, досым,
қалайсың!"). The seed rows were written by the team. So the "real-only" numbers in §9.3 measure
the model on human-written text that does not come from our templates. That is closer to real use
than the template cross-validation, but it is not a sample of real traffic. The first collected
messages (SAQ-19, SAQ-50) are needed before any claim about real-world recall.

## 12.5 Labels

Every message gets exactly one label.

| Label | Definition | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| `scam` | Uses deception to get money, a code, card or personal data. Includes messages that push the user to a link, a call or an app for that purpose | "Здравствуйте, служба безопасности банка… назовите код из смс для отмены" · "Мерейтойлық ұтыс! Сізге 4 000 000 тг бонус есептелді. Шығару үшін 3840 тг комиссия төлеңіз" |
| `suspicious` | Unsolicited advertising, pressure or a too-good offer, but no clear request for money, codes or data | "Только сегодня! Скидка 79% на все товары…" · "Қызметкерлер қажет, кесте еркін, күнделікті төлем 12 000 тг. Telegram +7XXXXXXXXXX" |
| `safe` | Personal or service messages with no such request, including genuine one-time codes that say not to share them | "Мама, купи хлеб и молоко пожалуйста" · "Tele2: тариф продлен, списано 2884 тг…" · "Сәлеметсіз бе, Әсел! Ертең сағат 12:00-де дәрігерге жазылғаныңызды еске саламыз" |

`scam` rows also get one **category**. The corpus marks non-scam rows as `legitimate` or
`grey_marketing`; `merge_datasets.py` empties those, because the classifier only predicts
categories for scams.

| Category | Definition | Example |
|---|---|---|
| `fake_bank` | Pretends to be a bank or its security service; asks for a code, card data, or a call back | "Сотрудник банка свяжется с вами… Не сообщайте код никому, кроме специалиста" |
| `phishing` | A link to a look-alike site of a bank, eGov, pension fund or operator to "confirm" or "receive" something | "eGov акkаунтыңыз жедел түрде жойылады. ЖСН расtaңыз: egov-kz.online" |
| `fake_prize` | A prize, bonus or compensation that needs a fee or personal data first | "Поздравляем! Вы выиграли 68 300 тг… Забрать приз: halykbank.kz.verify.top" |
| `fake_police` | Pressure from police, prosecutors, court bailiffs; a "safe account", a criminal case, an arrest of accounts | "Служба судебных исполнителей. Наложен арест на счета…" |
| `kaspi_scam` | Fake Kaspi payments, transfers, bonuses or Kaspi-branded codes with a call-back number | "Kaspi Gold: код… Егер бұл сіз болмасаңыз +7XXXXXXXXXX нөміріне қоңырау шалыңыз" |
| `other` | A scam that fits none of the above | — |

### Decision rules (apply in order)

1. Asks to tell, forward or type a code, PIN, CVV or password → **scam**. Category: `fake_bank`,
   or `kaspi_scam` if it is Kaspi-branded.
2. Asks to transfer money, pay a fee or move money to a "safe account" → **scam**. Category:
   `fake_police` for an authority, `fake_prize` for a prize or compensation.
3. Contains a look-alike domain of a bank, eGov, Kaspi or an operator, or asks to log in through a
   link → **scam**. Category: `phishing`, or `kaspi_scam` for a payment or transfer link.
4. Advertising, job offers, "you won" without asking for money or data, urgency without a request
   → **suspicious**.
5. Otherwise → **safe**.

### Edge cases

- A genuine one-time code from a known service ("Код: 1234. Никому не сообщайте") → **safe**.
  The same code followed by "if this was not you, call +7…" → **scam** (`kaspi_scam` or
  `fake_bank`): the call-back number is the trap.
- Delivery and pick-up codes → **safe**.
- A real bank's reminder with no link and no request → **safe**.
- If unsure between `scam` and `suspicious`, choose **suspicious** and write why in `note`.
- `lang` is the language of most words. Mixed Kazakh-Russian messages are common; do not drop them.

## 12.6 Labelling procedure and agreement

1. **Anonymise first.** Replace:
   - phone numbers with `+7XXXXXXXXXX`;
   - names with `[NAME]`;
   - card and account numbers with `[CARD]`;
   - links to personal pages with `[LINK]`.

   Keep amounts and scam domains (`kaspii.kz`, `egov-kz.online`): they are the signal. Raw messages
   never go into Git ([CONTRIBUTING §4](../CONTRIBUTING.md)).
2. **Two annotators, independently.** Danial and Asylbek label every batch without seeing each
   other's labels. At least 20% of each batch, and at least 100 messages, is labelled by both.
3. **Agreement.** Compute Cohen's κ on the three labels for the overlap, and separately on the
   category of rows both marked `scam`.
   - κ ≥ 0.70: the batch is accepted.
   - κ < 0.70: discuss the disagreements, refine §12.5 and relabel the batch.
4. **Resolve.** Disagreements are settled together; the final label goes in `label`, the reason in
   `note`.
5. **Log.** For every batch, record date, annotators, size, κ and changes to the guideline (a table
   at the end of this document).
6. **Freeze the test set.** The real test set (SAQ-22) is chosen before any model is tuned on the
   new data, and it is never used for training, templates or rules.
